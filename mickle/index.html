A finance tracker that holds only what you tell it.

Mickle records the income, spending, investments and budgets that you enter yourself. It never connects to your bank, reads your SMS, or touches your notifications. Every figure in it got there because you typed it — and it stays yours.

Get launch updates Read the privacy policy

What it does

Everything below works offline of your bank, because there is no bank connection at all.

  • Budgets and categories that show where the month actually went
  • Credit card cycles tracked against real statement dates
  • Printable reports and CSV export — your data leaves whenever you want
  • App lock with device biometrics
  • Permanent deletion enforced at the database, not just hidden
  • Free — no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases

On your data

Three commitments that shape every decision in the app.

Your data is not the product

Nothing is sold, shared, or brokered — a commitment written into the Terms without a liability cap, because it is entirely ours to keep.

Collect as little as possible

An email address and the entries you choose to log. No bank connections, no contact lists, no location, no advertising identifiers.

Leaving is as easy as joining

Full export in one tap, and permanent deletion that actually deletes. Software you cannot leave is software you cannot trust.
