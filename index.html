How the work is done

Four things that shape every engagement.

Public data, read properly

Government economic data for Assam, Dibrugarh district and Kamrup Metropolitan district — sourced openly, and interpreted rather than simply reprinted.

Local context, not templates

A recommendation that works in a metro often fails in Dibrugarh. Advice is shaped by the market the business is actually operating in.

Decisions, not documents

The output is something to act on — a clear call with the reasoning behind it — rather than a report that describes the situation back to the person already living it.

Billing you can predict

Consultations are charged, and any expenses incurred on a client's behalf appear as billables at cost. No surprises on the invoice.
